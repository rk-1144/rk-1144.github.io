Dijkstra's algorithm on an FPGA

Xilinx VivadoNexys 4 DDRDigital design

Shortest-path routing across the NIT Warangal campus map, computed in hardware instead of software.

  • Each location is stored as a custom 5-bit code, enough for 32 places with minimal logic.
  • Deployed on a Nexys 4 DDR board for real-time pathfinding.

Try it: click any two places on the sample graph. Blue marks the nodes Dijkstra visits; amber is the shortest path.
